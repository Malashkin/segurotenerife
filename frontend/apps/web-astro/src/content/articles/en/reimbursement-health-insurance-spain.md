Private healthcare in Spain comes in two formats: a "network" policy (cuadro médico), where you see doctors from the insurer's list, and a "reimbursement" policy (reembolso), where you choose any doctor yourself. The second gives the most freedom. Let us look at how it works.

## What a reimbursement policy is

With a reimbursement policy you **freely choose any doctor or clinic** — not just those in the insurer's network. You pay for the visit or procedure, and the insurer then **reimburses the cost** up to a set limit. Limits vary — for example, up to €100,000 a year, and more in higher tiers.

Such a policy usually includes full cover with hospitalisation, and often network access too — meaning it works in a mixed format: go through the network, or to any doctor with reimbursement.

## How it differs from a network policy

- **Network (cuadro médico):** only doctors from the list, but the visit is usually with no payment on the spot.
- **Reimbursement (reembolso):** any doctor and clinic, including private and abroad, but you pay first and are refunded afterwards.

## Who it suits

- Those who want a **specific doctor or clinic** not in the network.
- Those who value **freedom of choice** and are fine with the "pay and get refunded" format.
- Those who sometimes get treatment **in other countries** and want flexibility.

## What to look at

- **Reimbursement limit** — how much the insurer refunds per year.
- **Reimbursement percentage** — sometimes not 100% but a share is refunded.
- **Mixed option** — whether there is access to both network and reimbursement at once.
- **Waiting periods** — they can apply to complex services.

> Tip: reimbursement works well if you already have "your" doctor or want private clinics outside the network. If not paying on the spot matters more, look at the network option.

## How much it costs

Reimbursement policies are usually more expensive than network ones, due to the freedom of choice and high limits. The exact price is calculated individually — by age, cover and limit. **The estimate is free and commits you to nothing.**
