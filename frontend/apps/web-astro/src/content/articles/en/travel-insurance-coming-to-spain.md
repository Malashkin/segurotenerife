Not everyone coming to Tenerife needs a residency policy straight away. If you are coming for a short time — as a tourist, on a visit, or to scout before moving — travel insurance fits. Let us look at what it covers and where its limit is.

## What travel insurance for coming is

It is a policy for a **short stay** in Spain. It usually includes:

- medical assistance for illness or accident during the trip;
- protection of baggage and belongings;
- basic travel assistance.

It is a convenient and affordable policy — but for a temporary stay, not for living in the country.

## Who it suits

- **Tourists** — during a holiday in Tenerife.
- **Visitors** — relatives and friends coming for a few days.
- **Those coming to scout** before moving — on a reconnaissance trip, without arranging residency.
- **Short work visits** without relocating.

## How it differs from a residency policy

- **Travel insurance:** short term, travel assistance, baggage protection.
- **Residency policy:** full healthcare for a year, no copays or waiting periods, with a certificate for the consulate.

They are different tools for different needs. Travel insurance does not qualify for a visa — and conversely, there is no point arranging a yearly policy for a one-week trip.

## When to move to a residency policy

As soon as a **visa, residency or a move** comes into play, travel insurance is no longer enough — you need a full residency policy. If you are at that stage, take a look at our articles on residency insurance and the non-lucrative visa.

> Tip: if you are coming to "scout" and already thinking about moving, ask about both options at once — that way you can plan the budget in advance.
