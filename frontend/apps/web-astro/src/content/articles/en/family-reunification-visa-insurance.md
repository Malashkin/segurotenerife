If you already live legally in Spain, the next logical step is to bring your loved ones over. For that there is the family reunification visa (reagrupación familiar). One of the mandatory items is health insurance for each arriving relative. Let us look at the requirements.

## What family reunification is

Reagrupación familiar allows a Spanish resident to bring their family to join them. Usually this means:

- a spouse or registered partner;
- minor children (your own or under guardianship);
- dependent parents, under certain conditions.

The resident applicant proves sufficient income, housing and legal status, and arranges several documents for those arriving — including a health policy.

## The insurance requirement

Each family member being reunited needs a **private health policy** with the same conditions as for any residency:

- **full cover (sin copagos)** — no copays;
- **no waiting periods** — cover from day one;
- **for the whole year** and across all of Spain;
- **with a certificate** for immigration and the consulate.

## Particularities for the family

- **A policy per person.** Cover is needed for everyone arriving — adults and children alike.
- **Family policy.** It is often more convenient to include everyone under a single family policy: easier to manage and usually better value than separate ones.
- **Children.** For children, paediatrics and prevention matter — worth checking they are included.

> Tip: arrange the policies in advance so the certificates are ready when you submit the documents for the whole family at once — it speeds up the process.

## Common mistakes

- **Taking a travel policy** — it will not be accepted for reunification.
- **Not covering everyone** — a policy is needed for each relative.
- **Saving on a policy with copays** — the application may be refused.

## How much it costs

The cost depends on the number of family members, their age and cover, so it is calculated individually. **The estimate is free and commits you to nothing** — you can find out the amount for the whole family in advance.
