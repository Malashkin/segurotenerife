Tenerife attracts not only those moving for good but also students: universities, language schools, exchange programmes. If you are coming to study for more than three months, you will need a student visa — and with it, the right health insurance. Let us look at the requirements.

## When you need a student visa

A student visa is required to study in Spain for **more than 90 days** — whether at a university, college or long language courses. One of the mandatory documents is a private health policy.

## The insurance requirement

For a student visa the consulate requires a policy meeting the same strict conditions as for residency:

- **full cover (sin copagos)** — no copays for appointments or procedures;
- **no waiting periods** — cover from day one;
- **for the whole study period** and across all of Spain;
- **with a certificate for the consulate** confirming these conditions.

## Particularities for students

- **Duration = study duration.** The policy is arranged for the whole visa period; if you extend your studies, you extend the insurance too.
- **Age and price.** For young students policies are usually affordable — a plus.
- **Language courses.** If the course is longer than 90 days and a student visa is arranged, the policy requirements are the same as for university study.

> Tip: arrange the policy in advance so the certificate is ready when you submit documents — it is a common cause of delays.

## Common mistakes

- **Taking a travel policy** — it will not be accepted for a student visa.
- **Saving on a policy with copays** — the application may be refused.
- **Leaving it to the last minute** — better to do it early.

## How much it costs

For students insurance is usually among the affordable options, but the exact amount is calculated individually — by age, duration and cover. **The estimate is free and commits you to nothing.**
