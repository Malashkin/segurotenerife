Tenerife has long been a chosen destination for a calm retirement: a mild climate all year round, the sea and an unhurried pace. To make the move truly carefree, it is worth sorting out health insurance in advance — later in life it has its own particularities.

## Why a retiree needs a private policy

To arrange non-lucrative residency (the visa for people with their own means), private health insurance is mandatory: the consulate requires a policy with full cover, no copays and no waiting periods, valid across all of Spain.

But it is not only about the visa. A private policy gives what is especially valuable with age — fast access to doctors and diagnostics without long waits.

## What matters most at this age

When choosing a policy for older ages, it is worth looking at:

- **access to specialists** — cardiology, ophthalmology, orthopaedics and other areas without queues;
- **diagnostics** — tests, scans and check-ups on time;
- **hospitalisation** — both planned and emergency cases;
- **24/7 emergency care**;
- **language** — where possible, doctors who speak your language, so nothing is lost when describing symptoms.

## Particularities of arranging cover later in life

- **Higher cost.** With age the policy costs more — that is normal, but the cover is more substantial.
- **Health questionnaire.** The insurer may ask about chronic conditions. Answer honestly — it affects the terms.
- **Arrange it early.** The sooner the policy is in place, the simpler: some conditions are easier to lock in without delay.

> Tip: do not go by price alone. For a comfortable retirement, convenient access to the specialists you need and clear communication with your doctor matter more.
