It is not the easiest subject to talk about, but that is exactly why it is worth thinking through in advance — calmly and unhurried. Funeral insurance (seguro de decesos) is very common in Spain, and for an expat it has a special meaning. It is not about fear — it is about caring for your loved ones.

## What funeral insurance is

Seguro de decesos takes on **organising and paying for the funeral**, plus helping the family in a hard moment. In Spain it is a familiar and respected type of insurance: people take it out to spare their loved ones the arrangements and costs just when they are already struggling.

## Why it matters especially for an expat

Living far from your home country, we rarely think about the hardest scenario. But here the expat has a nuance:

- the policy can cover **repatriation to the home country**;
- the insurer handles the organisation, not disoriented relatives in a foreign country;
- many programmes also work **abroad** — for example, on trips of up to 90 days with a medical limit.

> For those living between countries, it is a way to be sure that, wherever something happens, loved ones will not be left alone with a hard process in another language.

## What it usually includes

- the full set of funeral services and free choice of funeral home;
- transfers and administrative matters;
- help with paperwork and inheritance formalities;
- legal advice;
- psychological support for the family.

## What it is really about

Funeral insurance is not about something grim. It is about care: you take off your loved ones, in advance, what would be hard for them to face at the moment of a loss. A quiet but very human decision.

## How much it costs

The cost depends on age and the chosen programme, so it is calculated individually. These policies are usually affordable, and **the estimate is free and commits you to nothing**.
