A dog in Tenerife means beaches, mountain trails and a great deal of contact with the neighbours. It also means one legal detail newcomers usually discover later than they should: **you are liable for the damage your pet causes**, with your own assets.

## What RC is, and why it is not about treating your dog

**Responsabilidad civil (RC)** is civil liability. The policy covers harm your dog causes to **third parties**:

- biting a person — treatment, compensation;
- injuring another animal — your neighbour's vet bills;
- damaging someone else's property.

Treatment for **your own** dog is not included: that is [veterinary insurance](/en/blog/pet-insurance-spain/), a separate product. They are often bought together, but they should not be confused.

## Who is required to have it

**Potentially dangerous breeds (PPP).** Here there is no ambiguity: civil liability cover is a mandatory condition. The owner obtains a **licence** from the town hall, and the RC policy sits in the document pack alongside the criminal record certificate and the medical report. No policy, no licence — and keeping such a dog without one is a fine.

The PPP category covers not only listed breeds but also dogs the municipality classifies that way on physical characteristics: weight, chest size, musculature, jaw shape. A crossbreed with no paperwork can therefore qualify as PPP.

**All other dogs.** Spain's animal welfare law introduced a civil liability insurance requirement for dog owners generally. How it is applied is set out in implementing rules and by individual municipalities, so **check the current position where you live** — in Tenerife, the ayuntamiento where you are registered.

> The practical takeaway: even where it is not formally required, an RC policy costs little and closes a risk that in the worst case runs into tens of thousands of euros.

## What else a dog owner in Tenerife needs

- **Microchipping and registration** in the municipal animal register.
- **Vaccinations** on the Canary schedule, recorded in the pet passport.
- For PPP — a **licence**, plus muzzle and a lead no longer than the permitted length in public.
- Beach rules: dogs are not allowed everywhere, there are designated "playas caninas".

## What it costs

An RC policy is one of the cheapest in the range: it covers liability rather than medical care, and the tariff depends mainly on whether the dog is classed as PPP and on the sum insured you choose. The exact figure is calculated per case, and **the quote is free**.

## How to arrange it

You need the dog's details (breed, age, microchip number) and your own as owner. For a PPP dog the policy is taken out before you apply for the licence — it is one of the required attachments.
