A familiar situation: you already live in Tenerife but do not work officially — a freelancer with no Spanish contributions, a retiree, the spouse of a resident. There is no automatic right to free public healthcare. Two routes remain: the **Convenio Especial** or a private policy. Let us look at both.

## What the Convenio Especial is

It is an agreement with your autonomous community (in Tenerife, the Canary Islands health service) under which you **pay a fixed monthly fee and gain access to public healthcare**: a family doctor, specialists by referral, hospitalisation, emergency care.

The fee is set by regulation, differs for people under and over 65 and is revised periodically — check the current amount with your own community.

## Who qualifies

The conditions are set nationally but processed regionally. The basic requirements are usually:

- you are **legally resident** in Spain and registered at your address (empadronamiento) for a set period — as a rule at least a year;
- you have **no entitlement** to healthcare on other grounds (employment, contributions, an agreement between countries);
- you are not covered by another country's compulsory system.

You apply to the regional health authority with proof of residence and registration.

## What it leaves out

This is the decisive part:

- **Medication at full price.** Residents with ordinary entitlement pay a subsidised share for prescriptions. A Convenio Especial holder pays **100%**. With ongoing treatment that can swallow the whole saving.
- **The basic public package only** — no additional programmes.
- **The same queues** as everyone else: specialists by GP referral, planned procedures by appointment. More on the difference in [private versus public healthcare](/en/blog/private-vs-public-healthcare-spain/).
- **Not valid** for a visa or residency application.

## When a private policy wins

Private insurance is the better answer if what you need is:

- **Speed.** Book a specialist directly, no referral — [how it works in Tenerife](/en/blog/private-healthcare-tenerife/).
- **Choice of doctor** and of language, through the [cuadro médico network](/en/blog/what-is-cuadro-medico-spain/).
- **Immigration paperwork.** For a [visa or residence permit](/en/blog/insurance-for-residency-visa/) only a private policy with the [right certificate](/en/blog/insurance-certificate-for-visa/) is accepted.
- **Dental and add-ons**, which the basic public package does not include.

> In practice many residents use both layers: the public system as the base for complex and expensive cases, a private policy for speed and everyday matters.

## How to choose

| Your situation | What to look at first |
|---|---|
| Applying for a visa or residence permit | A private policy — there is no alternative |
| Already resident, on permanent medication | Price the Convenio Especial including full-cost prescriptions |
| You want speed and choice of doctor | A private policy |
| You want cover across the full range of risks | A private policy, alongside the public system if needed |
